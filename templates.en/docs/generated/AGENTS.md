## Usage Rules

- Generated materials must note source, generation time, and purpose.
- Important confirmed conclusions should be promoted to `docs/product-specs/`, `docs/design-docs/`, `docs/references/`, or other long-term locations.
- Outdated generated materials should be deleted or moved to `docs/archive/` to avoid AI reusing stale content.
